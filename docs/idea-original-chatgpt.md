## 1. Global Fan Map – Idea & Business Case

### Product idea
Global Fan Map is an interactive sports platform that visualizes global fan support geographically.

The core experience is simple: a user opens a sports match and sees a world map showing which countries support each of the two competing teams (e.g. Liverpool vs Manchester City) — countries colored by which club has more votes, neutral countries in a neutral color.

Users can explore maps without voting. If they support one of the teams, they can vote for exactly one club in that match. Not primarily a prediction or betting app — a global fan sentiment and visualization platform.

### Core value proposition
For fans: see where their club is supported globally, compare against rivals, discover surprising countries/regions, participate with one simple vote, share match maps on social media.

For sports communities: highly shareable content ("Liverpool dominates Europe", "Man City leads support in India", "Arsenal wins the USA fan vote"). Naturally suited to Reddit, X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube communities.

### Core user loop
Discover match → Open match map → Explore global support → Vote for one club (optional) → See map update → Share result → Other fans visit → More votes and more interesting maps

## 2. Business Case

### The opportunity
No widely recognized simple platform focused on visualizing global fan allegiance country-by-country per match. Sits between sports data, fan communities, social media, interactive visualization. Low-friction core action: choose a team → vote → see the world map.

### Potential viral mechanism
Shareable result format: "Liverpool 62% / Manchester City 38% — see the global fan map." The map itself becomes the content. Acquisition channels: Reddit football communities, X football discussions, Facebook fan groups, football YouTube channels/podcasts, club-specific communities, sports pages on Instagram/TikTok.

### Initial market
Football: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, UEFA Champions League.
US sports: NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, MLS.
Football = visual and strategic core (strong international fan bases).

### Possible monetization later (not needed for MVP)
Advertising (sports-related once traffic exists), sponsored matches ("Global Fan Battle presented by X"), premium analytics (historical country support, fan growth by country, country vs country comparisons, historical match maps, club global fan profile), partnerships (sports media, football communities, podcasts, fan channels).

## 3. Product Philosophy
Should feel: premium, modern, visual, fast, simple, trustworthy.
Avoid: gambling appearance, betting aesthetics, clutter, complicated dashboards, excessive statistics.
The map is the hero.
